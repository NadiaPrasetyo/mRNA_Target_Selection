## Proteome-Based Approach

1. Obtain the proteome of *S. aureus*.
2. Define criteria for good antigens:
    - Non-homologous to human proteins
    - Cell-surface expressed
    - Immunogenic (B cell and T cell epitopes)
    - Functionally essential (less likely to mutate)
3. Retrieve protein sequences.
4. Run multiple sequence alignment to determine evolutionary or consensus sequences.

*Manual pipeline is also useful.*
