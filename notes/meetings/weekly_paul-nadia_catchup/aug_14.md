### Other Feature Ideas

- **Structural Analysis**:
    - AlphaFold3 (3D) + DSSP (2D)

- **Domain Identification**:
    - Adhesin domains, toxin domains from Pfam/InterPro
    - Motifs for host-cell binding (e.g., RGD, integrin-binding, lectin-like domains)

- **Immune Modulation**:
    - Avoid proteins that mimic host proteins excessively (risk of autoimmunity).
    - Consider targets involved in immune modulation, e.g., BIG CLANS (WD40, HTH, ZF, LRRs, etc.).

- **Genomic Considerations**:
    - Avoid hypervariable or recently acquired genomic islands (can reduce cross-strain conservation).
    - Focus on genes consistently present in the same genomic neighborhood across strains, as they may be functionally constrained.

- **Epidemiological Insights**:
    - Use genomic epidemiology data to identify slow-evolving targets for longer-term vaccine efficacy.

- **Tooling**:
    - Use HMMER to pull models from Pfam based on taxonomy 
    - InterPro
