## Suggestions for Obtaining Protein Sequences (Annotation-Independent)

- **Fetch protein sequences based on nucleotide sequence similarity:**  
    Use tools such as MMseqs (can run locally) or BLASTp as alternatives.

- **Analyze antigen proportions:**  
    Determine what proportion of proteins have antigens for a particular pathogen versus non-pathogen (e.g., cyanobacteria).

- **Include negative controls:**  
    Control for potential confounders such as sequence lengths and hydrophobicity.

- **Add a sanity check:**  
    Verify if the epitopes can be found in the protein sequences by obtaining the epitope sequences and cross-checking them with the protein sequences.
